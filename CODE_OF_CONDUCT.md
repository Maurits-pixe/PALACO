# Code of Conduct

Contributors must treat others respectfully in repository discussions,
reviews, issues and project spaces. Welcome different backgrounds and skill
levels; critique work rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and publication of
private information without consent are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to repository maintainers using an available
private contact channel. Do not post personal or sensitive details in public
issues. Maintainers should review reports fairly, protect confidentiality and
avoid conflicts of interest. Responses may include a warning, removal of
content or restrictions on participation according to severity and recurrence.
