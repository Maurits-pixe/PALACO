# VISITCARD-CONSENT-001
## User-Controlled Encounter v0.1

### Status
Draft v0.1

### Last Updated
2026-10-06

### Canonical statement
Discovery of a VisitCard does not establish accepted interaction or authorization.

## Encounter states
- ENCOUNTERED
- INTRODUCED
- RECOGNIZED
- ACCEPTED
- CONNECTED

## Consent rules
1. Encounter is passive; acceptance is explicit.
2. Recognition may occur without immediate connection.
3. Connection does not imply authorization.

## UX obligations
- clear “meet/connect” choice
- no hidden auto-join to sensitive contexts
- visible validity/verification indicators
